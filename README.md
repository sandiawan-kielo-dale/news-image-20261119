One 1024x1024 image summing up Thursday 19 November 2026 in six
colour-coded panels: a black masthead with the date, a short bold headline
and a plain-language summary for each story, and a footer listing the
sources. The image is drawn by a pure-Python renderer (`tools/render.py`)
using its own tiny rasterizer and 5x7 bitmap font — standard library only,
no network, deterministic bytes. `main.py` validates the PNG (signature,
dimensions, size) and copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` as `entry.png`.
`design/news.html` is a static visual mock of the same layout for human
review.

## The six stories

1. **Trump–Xi–Putin summit** — First-ever three-way summit in Shenzhen on
   the APEC sidelines; a 90-minute meeting and a joint declaration pledging
   dialogue on nuclear risks and stable energy flows. Ukraine and Iran were
   discussed; no major agreement beyond the declaration.
2. **ICJ: Myanmar genocide** — The UN's top court ruled Myanmar committed
   state-sponsored genocide against the Rohingya, the first ruling of its
   kind. About 10,000 were killed in the 2017 crackdown; the court ordered
   investigations, compensation for victims, and the return of displaced
   people.
3. **Bangladesh unrest** — Nobel laureate Muhammad Yunus's ancestral home
   was set ablaze; 4 people were killed as violence spread to 28 districts
   amid a nationwide opposition shutdown.
4. **Gulf / Iran war** — The US struck Iran's Qeshm island; a Houthi
   missile damaged the PIF Tower in Riyadh; Trump said the US would "see
   what happens" after 1 January 2027.
5. **Ukraine** — Russian strikes killed at least 5 people and hit power
   infrastructure, cutting electricity in Dnipropetrovsk, Zhytomyr,
   Zaporizhzhia, Mykolaiv and Sumy regions.
6. **COP31 climate talks** — Final-stage negotiations in Antalya, Turkey,
   stalled over fossil-fuel language ("transition away" vs phaseout) and
   climate finance.

Sources: AP, Reuters, DPA, Just Security, IANS.
