THE WORLD ON THURSDAY

19 NOVEMBER 2026

SUMMIT01

TRUMP-XI-PUTIN SUMMIT

The three leaders held a first-ever 90-minute meeting in Shenzhen and pledged dialogue on nuclear risks and stable energy flows. Ukraine and Iran were discussed, but no major deal was reached.

ICJ RULING02

MYANMAR GENOCIDE RULING

The UN's top court ruled Myanmar committed state-sponsored genocide against the Rohingya, the first ruling of its kind. About 10,000 were killed in 2017; the court ordered probes, compensation and return of the displaced.

BANGLADESH03

YUNUS HOME SET ABLAZE

Nobel laureate Muhammad Yunus's ancestral home was set ablaze as violence spread to 28 districts. Four people were killed amid a nationwide opposition shutdown.

GULF WAR04

US STRIKES IRAN; RIYADH HIT

The US struck Iran's Qeshm island, and a Houthi missile damaged the PIF Tower in Riyadh. Trump said the US would 'see what happens' after 1 January 2027.

UKRAINE05

STRIKES KILL 5, CUT POWER

Russian strikes killed at least five people and hit power infrastructure, cutting electricity in Dnipropetrovsk, Zhytomyr, Zaporizhzhia, Mykolaiv and Sumy regions.

COP3106

CLIMATE TALKS STALL

Final-stage talks in Antalya, Turkey, stalled over fossil-fuel language - 'transition away' versus phaseout - and climate finance.
